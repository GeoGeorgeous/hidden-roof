Privacy Policy

Last updated October 22, 2026

Like any website, our server sees your IP address when your browser downloads the game’s files. Beyond that, single player sends nothing about you; multiplayer needs a nickname. No cookies, accounts, analytics, ads or third-party resources, and we never sell data.

Who Is Responsible

The controller under the GDPR for the game Hidden Roof at roof.hidden.haus is Hidden Haus, operated by Georgios Gorchev, an individual developer based in the country of Georgia (“we”). Contact: roof@hidden.haus.

What We Process

  • On your device: the game keeps its own state on your device in localStorage (your settings, last nickname and progress, such as which hints you have already seen), so that the game works and shows you relevant hints, until you clear the site’s data; while you are in a multiplayer session, its code, a rejoin token and your nickname in sessionStorage, deleted when you leave or close the tab. This is strictly necessary for features you ask for, so it needs no consent (Article 5(3) ePrivacy Directive). Save and Upload work with files on your device only.
  • In multiplayer: your nickname, the session code, your position, view, tool, color and movement (about 20 times a second), the tools you carry, your paint and stepladder, and, when you host, the level and starting paint. Everyone in the session sees your nickname, avatar and paint. Do not put personal data in them.
  • On the server: your IP address with each connection, and the browser’s user agent with each request. The game server’s log records the time, session code, nickname, IP address and event (joined, left, dropped, turned away, error). The web server keeps no access log; its error log can hold an IP address.

Why, and on What Legal Basis

  • To run the game you ask for (Article 6(1)(b) GDPR): settings, session data and multiplayer.
  • Our legitimate interests (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR): IP addresses and logs to keep the server secure, limit connections per address, block abuse and fix faults; and recordings to promote the game (next section).
  • Legal obligations (Article 6(1)(c) GDPR): answering lawful requests from authorities.

You are not required to give us any data; multiplayer just cannot work without a nickname and a connection. We make no automated decisions about you.

Recordings

We may publish screenshots and video of multiplayer sessions to promote Hidden Roof and Hidden Haus, on our websites and on platforms such as YouTube, Instagram and TikTok, which process them under their own policies. They can show your nickname, avatar and paint. You can object at any time (see below).

How Long We Keep It

  • Sessions exist only in the server’s memory. A dropped player’s place is kept for 3 minutes; a session and its paint are deleted when the last player leaves or 3 minutes after the last one drops. An ended session’s code alone is kept for 1 hour.
  • The game server’s logs lose IP addresses and nicknames after 14 days and are deleted after 60 days. The web server’s error log is deleted after 14 days.
  • IP addresses blocked for abuse are kept for as long as the block is needed.
  • Recordings stay published; if you object, we remove your nickname from new material and, where we reasonably can, from our own posts (see below).
  • Copies others make, such as a paint file a player saves or a recording of a session, are theirs, not ours. Our deletion does not reach them.

Who Receives It

Other players in your session; our hosting provider, Hetzner Online GmbH, which runs the server in Nuremberg, Germany; the platforms where we publish recordings; and authorities where the law requires it. Players outside the EU receive your game data, and we run the server from the country of Georgia, both because it is needed to provide the game you asked for (Article 49(1)(b) GDPR).

Your Rights

You may ask for access to, correction, erasure, restriction or a portable copy of your data (Articles 15 to 18 and 20 GDPR), and complain to a data protection authority, for example in your EU country or the Personal Data Protection Service of Georgia. We have no accounts, so to find your data (Article 11(2) GDPR), tell us the date and time with time zone, the nickname and, if you can, the session code and your IP address. We answer within one month.

Your Right to Object

You may object at any time to processing based on our legitimate interests (Article 21 GDPR), including the use of your nickname in recordings. Email us the nickname and, if you can, the recording. We will keep it out of new material and, where we reasonably can, remove it from our own posts. We cannot remove copies that others have made or shared.

Age and Changes

The game is for people aged 18 and over. Before it processes anything this page does not describe, such as analytics or telemetry, we will update this page and announce it in the game.
