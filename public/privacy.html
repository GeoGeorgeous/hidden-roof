Privacy Policy

Last updated October 21, 2026

Single player sends nothing about you. Multiplayer needs a nickname, and the server sees your IP address. The game sets no cookies and has no accounts, analytics or ads.

Who Is Responsible

The controller under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (“GDPR”) for the browser game Hidden Roof at roof.hidden.haus (the “Service”) is:

  • Hidden Haus, operated by George Gorchev, an individual developer based in Georgia (“we” or “us”);
  • Email: roof@hidden.haus.

What the Service Does Not Do

  • It sets no cookies.
  • It has no analytics, telemetry, advertising or tracking.
  • It loads nothing from third parties: fonts, scripts and images come from roof.hidden.haus itself.
  • It has no accounts, and never asks for your name, email or password.
  • It does not sell or share personal information, including as defined by the California Consumer Privacy Act.

On Your Device

The game keeps the following in your browser. None of it is sent to us, except the session details described in the next section when you play multiplayer.

  • localStorage: your settings (look, graphics, detail, frame rate and key bindings) and the last nickname you typed for multiplayer. They stay until you clear the site’s data in your browser.
  • sessionStorage: while you are in a multiplayer session, its code, a random rejoin token and your nickname, so that reloading the page puts you back in the session. They are deleted when you leave the session or close the tab.
  • Your paintings: single-player paint stays in the page’s memory. Save downloads it as a file to your device, and Upload reads a file from your device into the game; neither sends it to us.

This storage is strictly necessary for features you ask for (your settings, rejoining a session), so it needs no consent under Article 5(3) of the ePrivacy Directive (2002/58/EC) and the national laws implementing it, such as § 25(2) of the German TDDDG.

When You Play Multiplayer

Hosting or joining a session opens a connection to our server. While you play, your browser sends:

  • your nickname and the five-letter session code;
  • about 20 times a second, your position, view direction, tool, color, nozzle and movement (crouching, on a ladder), and whether the game’s tab is in the background;
  • the tools and colors you carry, your paint as you make it, and where you put your stepladder;
  • when you host: the level, and the paint to start from if you choose your current painting or a paint file.

Everyone in the session sees your nickname, avatar, movements, stepladder and paint live. Do not put real names, contact details or other personal data in your nickname or paint.

The Server and Its Logs

Like any website, our server sees your IP address with each connection. The web server, which delivers the game’s files and forwards multiplayer connections, also sees the address requested, your browser’s user agent and the time. It keeps no access log of requests; its error log can contain an IP address when a connection fails.

The game server keeps a diagnostic log. Each line can hold the time, session code, nickname and player number, your IP address, and the event: joined, came back, left, dropped, turned away, or an error. It also logs summaries of sessions, never their paint.

We use IP addresses only to run connections, to apply limits per address against abuse (for example, the number of connections and hosted sessions from one address), to block abusive players, and to find faults. We do not use them to find out who you are.

Recordings for Promotion

We may capture screenshots and video of multiplayer sessions and publish them to present, promote and advertise Hidden Roof and Hidden Haus: in trailers, short videos, social media posts, advertisements and on our websites. They can show your nickname, avatar and paint. The platforms we publish on (such as YouTube, Instagram and TikTok) process the published material under their own privacy policies.

The legal basis is our legitimate interest in showing and promoting the game (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR). You can object at any time; see “Your Right to Object” below.

Legal Bases

  • To provide the Service you ask for (Article 6(1)(b) GDPR): your settings and session details on your device, and your nickname, session code, game state and paint on the server.
  • Our legitimate interests (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR): keeping the Service secure and working, through IP addresses, connection limits and logs; and promoting the game through recordings. We consider these interests not to override yours, because the data is limited, kept briefly and never used to identify you.
  • Legal obligations (Article 6(1)(c) GDPR): answering lawful requests from authorities or courts.

Neither the law nor a contract requires you to give us personal data. Single player needs none. Multiplayer cannot work without a nickname and a connection, which sends your IP address.

We make no decisions about you by automated means that have legal or similarly significant effects. Connection limits are applied automatically per IP address only to protect the server.

How Long We Keep It

  • Sessions exist only in the server’s memory and are never written to disk. If your connection drops, your place is kept for 3 minutes so you can come back. A session and its paint are deleted when the last player leaves, or 3 minutes after the last player drops. A copy of the paint made for a joining player or a save is kept for at most 15 seconds. After a session ends, only its code is remembered, for 1 hour, so that late arrivals are told it has ended. A server restart deletes all sessions.
  • Logs of the game server and the web server are deleted after 14 days. A specific entry may be kept longer only while we need it to deal with an attack or a legal claim.
  • Recordings stay published for as long as they are useful for promoting the game, unless you object.
  • Data on your device stays until you clear it, as described above.

Who Receives It

  • Other players in your session, as described above.
  • Our hosting provider, Hetzner Online GmbH, Germany, runs the server in Nuremberg, Germany, and processes the data on our behalf under a data processing agreement (Article 28 GDPR).
  • Viewers of our recordings and the platforms we publish on.
  • Authorities or courts, only where the law requires it.

Outside the EU. The server is in Germany. Other players can connect from any country, and receive your game data because you chose to play with them (Article 49(1)(b) GDPR). We are based in Georgia, which has no EU adequacy decision, and run the server from there over encrypted connections, as needed to provide the Service (Article 49(1)(b) GDPR).

Your Rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access your data (Article 15), to have it corrected (Article 16) or erased (Article 17), to restrict its processing (Article 18), and to receive data you gave us in a portable format (Article 20). You can also lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority, in particular in the EU country where you live or work, or with the Personal Data Protection Service of Georgia.

Email roof@hidden.haus; we answer within one month. Because the Service has no accounts, we cannot find your data by your name or email. To let us find it (Article 11(2) GDPR), tell us the approximate date and time with time zone, the session code if you know it, the nickname you used, and your IP address at the time if you can.

Your Right to Object

You may object at any time, on grounds relating to your particular situation, to processing based on our legitimate interests (Article 21 GDPR), including the use of your nickname in recordings we publish. Email roof@hidden.haus with the nickname and, if you can, the recording. We will not show your nickname in new material, and will remove it from our own published posts where we can, unless we have compelling legitimate grounds or need the data for a legal claim.

Age

The Service is for people aged 18 and over and is not directed at children. If you believe a child has played multiplayer, email us and we will delete their data from the logs.

Changes

We update this policy when the Service changes. Before we add anything it does not describe, such as analytics or performance telemetry, we will update this page and announce the change in the game. The date at the top shows the current version.
