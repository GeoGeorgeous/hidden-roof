your own keys, new tool controls, a performance test, and multiplayer that holds on through a bad connection.

- **performance test**
  Settings → Graphics has a performance test: a 10 second flight around the level, with live numbers, then your frame rate, 1% low, CPU and GPU time, and a tip for your settings. Esc stops it, Enter runs it again.

- **your graphics card**
  Settings names the graphics card the game draws with. on a laptop that uses the built-in one, it says how to give the browser the faster card. Frame rate says what it does above your screen's refresh rate.

- **your controls**
  Settings → Controls lets you rebind every action, two keys each. your settings are now remembered between visits.
  - a mouse wheel step can't be bound to an action you hold (move, jump, crouch, run, use, shake)
  - turning the performance lines on is remembered
  - the arrow keys move you too
  - crouch (hold or toggle) and tap twice to run are set there
  - reset all puts back every key, with an undo

- **color picker**
  click the mouse wheel on any paint to switch to the closest color you carry.

- **tool controls**
  the mouse wheel changes the color, Q/E change the size. hold Q/E to keep going.
  - **can**: Q/E cycle through your caps
  - **marker, roller, sponge**: Q/E change the width

- **multiplayer**
  if you drop out, you have 3 minutes to come back as yourself, with your stepladder and what you carried. strokes you made as the connection died are painted again once you're back.
  - joining a session with a lot of paint is faster and no longer freezes the game
  - while you join or host, the menu shows how far the paint has loaded instead of PLAY; LEAVE cancels
  - the server no longer says it's full right after big sessions end
  - other players move smoothly on a bad connection
  - a connection that is slow only one way no longer drops
  - paint takes much less traffic
  - a reloaded or duplicated tab keeps the paint it sends
  - SAVE while reconnecting says to save again once you're back, instead of waiting forever
  - leaving while a session loads no longer leaves PAINT DETAIL locked, or says you're in a session with no connection
  - a game error ends the session with "the game hit an error", not "a new version is out"
  - players on 1.3.0 can't join: reload to update

- **the browser**
  while you play, the keyboard is the game's: browser shortcuts and zoom no longer get in the way, and the mouse's back and forward buttons stay in the game. F5, F11 and Esc still work, in fullscreen too.

- **smoother**
  placing the stepladder no longer hitches, and a page zoomed out in the browser no longer draws more pixels than your screen has. paint cleaned off with the sponge gives its memory back, also when another player cleans it.

- **fixes**
  - you can stand on water tank roofs
  - shaking and spraying the can no longer work at once
  - either Shift runs
  - a trackpad swipe changes one color at a time
  - K takes a screenshot only while you play
  - on a Mac, keys no longer stick after a Cmd shortcut
  - a game opened in a background tab loads
  - the loading screen says why when the game can't start
  - if something breaks while you play, the game says so and offers a reload, instead of freezing
  - paint runs trickle the same at any frame rate, so other players see them closer to how you do
  - the PLAY button shows its ESC key in Firefox and Safari, where Esc resumes
  - a trackpad swipe's sideways part no longer drops part of a color step
