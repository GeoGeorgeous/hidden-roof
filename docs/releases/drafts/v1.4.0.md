new tool controls, and multiplayer that holds on through a bad connection.

- **tool controls**
  the mouse wheel changes the color, Q/E change the size.
  - **can**: Q/E cycle through your caps
  - **marker, roller, sponge**: Q/E change the width

- **multiplayer**
  if you drop out, you have 3 minutes to come back as yourself, with your stepladder and what you carried. strokes you made as the connection died are painted again once you're back.
  - joining a session with a lot of paint is faster and no longer freezes the game
  - other players move smoothly on a bad connection
  - a connection that is slow only one way no longer drops
  - paint takes much less traffic
  - players on 1.3.0 can't join: reload to update

- **smoother**
  placing the stepladder no longer hitches, and a page zoomed out in the browser no longer draws more pixels than your screen has.

- **fixes**
  - you can stand on water tank roofs
  - shaking and spraying the can no longer work at once
  - a game opened in a background tab loads
  - the loading screen says why when the game can't start
  - typing a K in the session code no longer takes a screenshot
