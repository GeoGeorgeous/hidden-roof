- **hints**
  - short lines at the bottom of the screen teach each control when you need it: moving, every tool, colors, ladders, low pressure; one at a time, gone once you've done it, and your rebound keys show as bound
  - Settings → Gameplay: turn hints off, or show them again from the start
  - pickups, joins and leaves show as short messages above the hints, up to three at once, none lost while the menu is open or the tab is hidden; the same message twice in a row shows once
  - a cleaner screen: only the body-cam frame, no REC, camera label or clock; the performance lines stay off until you turn them on in Settings

- **multiplayer**
  - leaving a session takes you back to the title screen with your own paint, as it was before you joined; the session's paint stays only if you saved it (a session lost for good shows a dialog saying why, with a button that saves the paint to a file before leaving, reloads the page after an update, or opens the menu; Esc just leaves)
  - a reconnecting status with animated bars turns into a "Reconnected" check when the link is back, and grows into the dialog when the time to come back runs out; coming back online retries at once
  - a status says when the server goes quiet (no signal) or the round trip is long (bad connection)
  - a player's name tag shows what they're doing with a small icon: away, in the menu, idle, far behind, no signal or disconnected; far-off tags fade
  - when the game itself crashes, a dialog shows the error and its stack to copy, and offers to reload

- **paint runs**
  - paint dries after a few seconds: spraying over a dry coat or a rolled wall no longer starts runs, holding the can on one spot still does
  - fewer runs: the roller lays a lighter coat, and caps run less, from the skinny cap (hardly) to the fat cap (most)

- **performance**
  - other players spraying cost far less: a full session of cans no longer drops your frame rate
  - moving the stepladder around near lamps no longer slows the game: the stepladder is lit at once, the light around it settles a moment after it stops

- **the site**
  - links to the game shared in chats and on social sites show its picture, title and description; the game is installable with its own icon
  - Terms and Privacy pages, linked from the pause menu and the small-screen page: no cookies, no analytics, no accounts
  - the pause menu links the game's Discord, GitHub, YouTube, Instagram and TikTok; search engines and AI readers find them too
  - the Terms and Privacy pages look like the rest of the menu: dark, with the game's title font
  - the Terms and Privacy pages are now full legal texts, with an email for requests: roof@hidden.haus
  - the Terms and Privacy pages say who runs the game, that players are 18+, and how to keep your nickname out of the videos we publish
  - streaming and videos of your play are allowed, monetized too
  - the server's log forgets your IP address and nickname after 14 days, and is deleted after 60; its daily file is capped in size and a client's text can't forge a log line
  - a nickname can no longer hold a line break
  - without JavaScript, the page says the game needs it instead of loading forever
  - Added a "Buy me a coffee" link in the pause menu footer. If you enjoy Hidden Roof, you can support it on Ko-fi.

- **fixes**
  - the first moments after loading no longer stutter while the game compiles its last shaders
  - screenshots (K) show the other players' name tags
