hints teach the controls as you play, multiplayer says what is going on with your link and with the other players, and paint dries, so a coat over dry paint no longer runs.

- **hints**
  - short lines at the bottom of the screen teach each control when you need it: moving, every tool, colors, ladders, low pressure; one at a time, gone once you've done it, and your rebound keys show as bound
  - Settings → Gameplay: turn hints off, or show them again from the start
  - pickups, joins and leaves show as short messages above the hints, up to three at once, none lost while the menu is open or the tab is hidden; a message shown again replaces the one on screen
  - key names follow your keyboard: on a Russian or other non-Latin layout they show as W, A, S, D
  - a cleaner screen: only the body-cam frame, no REC, camera label or clock; the performance lines stay off until you turn them on in Settings

- **multiplayer**
  - a player's name tag shows their nickname, what they're doing (away, in the menu, idle, lagging, no signal, disconnected: the connection troubles in color) and how far they are; far-off tags fade, and the nearest is drawn on top
  - a reconnecting status with animated bars turns into a "Reconnected" check when the link is back; coming back online retries at once
  - a status says when the server goes quiet (no signal, a blinking red ring) or the round trip is long (bad connection, a flickering bar)
  - a session lost for good shows a dialog saying why, with a button that saves the paint to a file before leaving, reloads the page after an update, or opens the menu; Esc leaves without saving
  - leaving a session takes you back to the title screen with your own paint, as it was before you joined; the session's paint stays only if you saved it
  - a nickname is Latin letters and digits only (A–Z, a–z, 0–9, up to 16): no spaces, punctuation or other alphabets; a second player with the same name becomes geo2
  - when the game itself crashes, a dialog shows the error to copy, and offers to reload

- **paint runs**
  - paint dries after a few seconds: spraying over a dry coat or a rolled wall no longer starts runs, holding the can on one spot still does
  - fewer runs: the roller lays a lighter coat, and caps run less, from the skinny cap (least) to the fat cap (most)

- **the site**
  - Terms and Privacy pages, linked from the pause menu and the small-screen page: no cookies, no analytics, no accounts; an email for requests: roof@hidden.haus
  - players are 18+; streaming and videos of your play are allowed, monetized too; you can ask us to leave your nickname out of the videos we publish
  - the server's log forgets your IP address and nickname after 14 days, and is deleted after 60
  - the pause menu links the game's Discord, GitHub, YouTube, Instagram and TikTok, and a "buy me a coffee" link to support the game on Ko-fi
  - links to the game shared in chats and on social sites show its picture, title and description; the game has its own app icon
  - without JavaScript, the page says the game needs it instead of loading forever

- **performance**
  - other players spraying cost less: the game reuses where their spray hits while their aim holds still
  - moving the stepladder around near lamps no longer slows the game: the stepladder is lit at once, the light around it settles a moment after it stops
  - the first moments after loading no longer stutter while the game compiles its last shaders

- **fixes**
  - screenshots (K) show the other players' name tags
