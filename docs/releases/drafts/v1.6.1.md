fair play on the public server: the Terms say how moderation works, and a ban tells you why; plus a few fixes.

- **fair play**
  - the [Terms](/terms.html) now say how moderation works: what we can do, how you're told, and how to report a player or appeal by email
  - a player who breaks the rules can be banned from multiplayer for a while or for good; the server list and the game show the reason and when it ends
  - while banned, single player stays open
  - a stepladder can't stand in an open doorway or right in front of it or behind it, so nobody can block the way in

- **about the game**
  - esc → about → game opens a page on what the game is, how to play it and the faq, right over your paused game
  - the settings' tabs read as the menu does: general (was gameplay), audio (was sound), controls, graphics
  - on a phone, the site shows that page in place of asking for a bigger screen
  - the site's page shows how to start playing on a computer, with a button to send the link to yourself; widen a narrow window and Play now appears
  - when the game can't start (WebGL2 turned off, a file that didn't load), the site says what to try, with a button to reload
  - make the game's window too small and a note asks for a bigger one; the game waits and goes on once it fits
  - the page says in the developer's own words what the game is, and its faq says what to do when the game runs slowly: the performance test, a laptop's power saver, the faster video card; its questions are in groups and open with a click
  - every update's notes are on the site too, at [roof.hidden.haus/updates.html](/updates.html)

- **fixes**
  - on a Mac, holding Esc in fullscreen tells you ⌃ ⌘ F leaves it, not F11
  - pickup rings on the title screen have the right size
