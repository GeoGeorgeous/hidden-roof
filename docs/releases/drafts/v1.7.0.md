the can paints like a real one.

- **the can**
  a stroke is now one line of paint, not a cloud of dots.
  - paint lands at once under the aim, with no gaps however fast the hand moves
  - hold it still and the spot darkens, then runs, with any cap: the wider the cap, the more it runs, and the more paint, the longer the run
  - a fast hand leaves a lighter, wider, softer line; a slow one a dense, crisp line
  - up close the line is narrow and hard; from far away, wide and soft
  - **opacity**: hold Shift and scroll with the can in hand to set it, from 5% to 100%: passes add up, so you can build a fade
  - the crosshair is as wide as the line you'd leave right now: it follows the cap, the distance and your hand
  - an arc beside the crosshair shows the opacity, with its percent as you change it
  - the crosshair fades where the paint wouldn't reach
  - the cap's and the color's tags sit by the cap and invert what's under them, as the crosshair does
  - the can no longer loses pressure or sputters: shaking it is just for the feel
  - paint is smooth now: see-through strokes no longer fall into hard bands
  - the spray is a soft plume at the nozzle now, and each cap has its own: a thin jet, a wide cloud
  - the plume follows your opacity: a faint can sprays a faint plume, and others see it that way
  - spray a wall at an angle and the paint stretches along your aim and fades at the far end, and everyone sees it that way

- **caps**
  caps take real names and colors: BANANA, NY FAT and ASTRO FAT are the ones you know.
  - **needle**: thinner than skinny, with its tube
  - **blue dot**: the softest edge, for fades
  - both are new: look for them on the roof

- **marker and roller**
  - they run as the can does: go over paint that's still wet and it may start a run, each tool with odds of its own

- **sound**
  - the city below is a real night now, recorded on a roof, and fades in as you start

- **settings**
  - General is in groups now, as Controls is
  - General, Audio and Graphics have a Reset all too: it takes back that tab alone
  - painting has settings of its own: a slower mouse and a narrower view while you paint, and Streamline, how smooth your lines are

- **fixes**
  - lightning with the thunder slider at 0 no longer stops the game
