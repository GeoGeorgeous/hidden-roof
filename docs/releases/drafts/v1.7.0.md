a whole new feel to painting, reworked audio, improved hints, and a few extra goodies for your next rooftop run.

- **painting**
  - **a new way to paint**: strokes now lay down continuous lines of paint instead of clouds of dots: paint lands instantly under your aim, with no gaps, no matter how fast you move
  - **speed and coverage**: move fast for lighter, wider, softer lines; slow down for denser, sharper ones. hold your aim in one spot and paint builds up, darkens, then starts to run: wider caps run more, heavier coverage runs longer
  - **distance matters**: get up close for narrow, crisp lines, or step back for wider, softer ones
  - **opacity (pressure)**: hold Shift and scroll with the can in hand to set it from 5% to 100%: each pass builds on the last, so you can work up a fade
  - **streamline**: smooths your lines as the brush trails your aim a little, set it in Settings → General
  - **a smarter crosshair**: it shows the line you're about to leave, adjusting to your cap, distance and hand speed, and your opacity (pressure) too, and it fades where the paint won't reach
  - **spray you can see**: every cap has its own soft plume at the nozzle, from a thin jet to a wide cloud: it follows your opacity, and other players see it too. spray at an angle and paint stretches along your aim, fading towards the far end

- **caps**
  six caps to choose from, each with its own feel, from fine details to big fills.
  - **NEEDLE (new)**: ultra-fine lines for sharp details and highlights
  - **SKINNY**: clean tags and crisp outlines
  - **BANANA**: a versatile all-rounder for everyday painting
  - **BLUE DOT (new)**: soft edges for smooth blends and fades
  - **NY FAT**: wide lines for throw-ups and quick fills
  - **ASTRO FAT**: massive coverage for big fills and backgrounds

- **marker, roller, sponge**
  - the marker and the roller can trigger runs in wet paint too, just like the can
  - the crosshair shows the actual size of all three on the wall and fades where they wouldn't reach

- **hints**
  - hints now sound more natural and use first-person phrasing: "I can spray with LMB"
  - each new cap comes with a quick tip the first time you put it on
  - each hint or message shows how much time it has left
  - action hints fill up as you do what they ask, then finish with a checkmark
  - walking, jumping and running each have their own hint, shown one after another and completed by doing the action
  - opacity and color hints stick around until you've actually changed them

- **sound**
  real recordings and a better mix to bring the rooftops to life.
  - **city**: the city below is now a real nighttime recording captured from a rooftop, fading in as you start
  - **rain and thunder**: real storm recordings, with a low rumble of heavy rain underneath and distant thunder rolling now and then, sometimes with no flash. when lightning strikes, three real thunder rolls follow a few seconds later, softer the farther away the strike is
  - **spray**: the can's hiss now responds to your opacity, distance from the wall and hand movement
  - **interiors**: step into a room and the city gets muffled, while the rain changes to the sound of rain hitting a tin roof

- **settings**
  - the General tab is now organized into groups, just like Controls
  - General, Audio and Graphics each have a Reset all option that resets only the current tab
  - Storm and Rain indoors now have their own sliders in Audio
  - painting gets its own settings: lower mouse sensitivity and a narrower field of view while painting

- **fixes and improvements**
  - no more pressure loss or sputtering: shaking the can is just... for vibes?
  - paint blends smoothly now, so translucent strokes don't break into hard bands
  - cap and color labels now sit next to the can, inverting whatever's behind them just like the crosshair
  - climbing down a ladder now counts for its hint, before only climbing up worked
  - Drops on metal is gone as a separate sound: those drops are now part of the rain
  - the can, your footsteps and the fans are quieter, leaving more room for the rain and the city
  - lightning lights up the sky and the moonlight more
  - capture mode has a clearer panel: one control a line, and in the free camera hold Tab for all its keys
  - the new caps are placed on the level, go find them
  - lightning no longer crashes the game when the Thunder slider is set to 0
