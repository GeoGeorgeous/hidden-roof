one public roof, open around the clock: its paint stays from one visit to the next.

- **public roof**
  - one public session runs all the time, on the roof at ULTRA detail
  - its paint is kept: saved on the server, and back after a restart
  - a release that changes the roof wipes its paint

- **server list**
  - multiplayer opens a list of every session: public and private, with players, ping and the tools each allows
  - a session has a name, shown in the list
  - a public session: read the house rules once and join, no code needed
  - the house rules say that breaking them may mean a ban
  - a private session: type its 5-letter code; the list shows which session it is before you join
  - a loading screen while the roof and its paint come in, with cancel
  - a full session puts you in line: see your place, and drop in as soon as a spot opens; lose your connection and your place is kept for 3 minutes

- **tools off**
  - a session can have tools turned off when it is made: their items leave the map, and nobody there can hold or use them
  - leave the session and everything is back

- **private sessions**
  - a private session takes up to 4 players
  - hosting your own session is off for now
  - the admin can remove a player from a session: the game says so, and they can join again
