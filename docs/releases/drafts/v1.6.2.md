capture mode: film the game in first person or with a free camera.

- **capture mode**
  - N turns it on and off, in a session too
  - first person: you play as ever, with the hud but the hints and the hotbar
  - free camera: no hud, you fly anywhere with W A S D, Q and E, the wheel sets the speed and Z and X the mouse smoothing
  - mark points with K and press P: the camera flies through them, turning as it goes
  - M records a video of the game with its sound and saves it when you stop, B picks its quality
  - F picks the frame: 16:9, 9:16 or 1:1
  - the controls show on screen, never in the video
  - V switches between first person and the free camera, and every key can be changed in settings → controls
  - in a session, the others see Capture by your name while you fly the free camera

- **fixes**
  - the rain on the page about the game plays on an iPhone, and the video is sharper
  - pickups' rings no longer grow when seen from far away
  - the ESC menu of a full session shows the first few players and +N, not a row running off the screen
  - a player with the game minimized is now seen by those who join or reload after that, not only in the menu
  - a player who minimizes the game while joining is seen too
  - a long time without a connection, painting all the way, no longer leaves you reconnecting for ever
  - a slow reload keeps your place for 30 seconds instead of 15
  - a stepladder placed or taken away while the connection is lost now reaches the others
  - no stepladder left behind that only you can see after a server restart
