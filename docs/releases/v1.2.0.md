multiplayer for up to 20 players, a busier roof with new windows, and the controls painted on the walls.

- **multiplayer**
  - up to 20 players in a session
  - you see the others' caps and spray, and who's away or disconnected
  - a session keeps your inventory when you come back
  - joining shows its progress

- **hints on walls**
  the controls are painted on the wall by the spawn. it's real paint: scrub it off or paint over it.

- **roof**
  the roof is busier and more realistic.
  - brick walls, more windows, lamps, signs, ducts, railings and skylights
  - caps, colors and tools are easier to tell apart
  - some windows are clear: see through them or paint over them

- **fixes and performance**
  - signs and billboards are lettered on the front only and read the right way
  - you no longer catch on railing posts
  - the mast can't be climbed
  - paint comes back after the graphics driver resets
  - a loading screen until the game is ready, so nothing freezes when you start
