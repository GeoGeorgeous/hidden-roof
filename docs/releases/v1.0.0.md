# Hidden Roof 1.0.0

the first release. you climb onto a hidden rooftop. it rains, neon flickers, and wires and billboards fill the city below. find your tools, paint, scrub it off and paint again.

  <img width="1200" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/dd699458-3ab9-4cce-ad3b-13989aeecef9" />

- **look**
  the whole world is drawn in ink, like a manga page: hatching and cross-hatching for shadows, pen outlines and paper-white light. the only color in the city is the paint you bring. lamp light and its shadows are baked in, and fog and lightning are drawn over the top.

- **painting**
  paint soaks into walls, floors and props and stays there. it crosses the seams between props, so one piece can run across a wall, a pipe and a door. if you lay on too much, it runs down the wall. the can loses pressure as you spray: shake it. paint never runs out.

- **tools**
  - **spray can**: your main tool, used with the caps below
  - **marker**: a square nib for outlines and handstyles
  - **roller**: solid bands of paint for big fills and blockbusters
  - **sponge**: scrubs paint off
  - **stepladder**: place it to reach higher walls

- **caps**
  - **standard**: an all-round cap for fills and outlines
  - **skinny**: thin, sharp lines for tags and details
  - **fat**: wide strokes for throw-ups and fast bombing
  - **spray**: a soft, wide spray for fades and backgrounds

- **multiplayer**
  host a session and share its 5-letter code to paint the same roof with a friend.

- **saves**
  save your work to a file and load it later. old saves still load after updates.
