capture mode and bug fixes.

- **capture mode**
  - press N to film the game in first person or with a free camera
  - in a session, the others see Capture badge by a person who captures

- **fixes**
  - the video on the page about the game is sharper and plays on phones
  - pickups' rings no longer grow when seen from far away
  - the menu shows session players correctly without running off the screen
  - players with the game minimized, even while joining, are now seen by everyone
  - painting offline for a long time no longer leaves you reconnecting for ever, and the paint reaches the others in order
  - a slow reload keeps your place for 30 seconds, not 15
  - stepladders stay in sync after a lost connection or a server restart
  - after a slow load, the others are no longer shown seconds late
