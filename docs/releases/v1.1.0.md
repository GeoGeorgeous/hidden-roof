a new roof to paint, a new look, more colors and smoother movement.

- **new roof**
  the game now starts on a new roof level, with its own spawn point and hidden pickups.

- **3 shader styles**
  pick one in settings.
  - new manga (the default)
  - old manga
  - retro light

- **12 colors**
  lime, teal, cyan and brown are now hidden on the roof too, for 12 colors to find.

- **sprint**
  double-tap W to sprint, like in Minecraft. you sprint until you let go of W.

- **can**
  hold RMB to shake the can. a tap gives back a little pressure, and holding it refills the can fast.

- **world**
  the aviation light on the dome now pulses red and lights the roof around it.

- **performance**
  the roof draws far fewer objects per frame and uses about a third less memory for paint. the game also loads faster.

- **fixes**
  pickups float in their rings again on the start screen.
