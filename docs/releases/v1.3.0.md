a new menu and a better roof with fewer rough edges.

- **menu**
  the pause screen, settings, multiplayer, save and upload are redesigned, with a credits page and an updates page.
  - hold esc to leave fullscreen: a ring shows how long
  - a Discord link

- **multiplayer**
  pausing in a live session shows your ping, the code to copy and everyone's initials in their paint colors.

- **roof**
  - security cameras tilt up and down to follow you, not only turn
  - the traffic cone takes paint on its outside
  - rain, neon flicker and volumetric light have new defaults
  - some pickups moved

- **fixes**
  - spray starts at the can
  - ladders end at their top, and scaffolding ladders sit in the middle of the hatch
  - the game's files start loading with the page
