Hidden Roof — a free multiplayer graffiti game in your browser

Hidden Roof is a free multiplayer graffiti game in your browser — paint ink-drawn rooftops alone or with friends.

You climb onto the rooftops of a city drawn in pen and ink, and the only color in it is the paint you bring. There are no enemies and no goals: find your tools, paint whatever you like, scrub it off, paint again.

What you need

  • A desktop or laptop browser with WebGL2
  • A keyboard and a mouse
  • A window at least 820×560
  • Nothing to download or install, and no account

Phones and tablets aren’t supported yet: open roof.hidden.haus on your computer.

How to play

You start with a spray can and black paint. The other tools, the caps and twelve more colors are hidden around the roof: find them, then paint the walls, the signs and the floor. Paint never runs out, and the sponge scrubs anything off.

  • Spray can: four caps, skinny for tags, standard, fat for fills, spray for a soft mist
  • Marker: crisp lines for outlines and details
  • Roller: big areas, fast
  • Sponge: scrubs paint off
  • Stepladder: reaches the walls no one else has touched

Controls

  • Move: W A S D; run: Shift; jump and climb: Space; crouch: Ctrl or C
  • Spray or use: left mouse button; shake the can: right mouse button
  • Next or previous color: mouse wheel; pick the color under the crosshair: middle mouse button
  • Bigger or smaller cap: E and Q; tools: 1 to 5
  • Screenshot: K; pause and settings: Esc. Every key can be changed in the settings

Multiplayer

Pick a session from the server list and paint the same roof with everyone in it, live. A public session takes up to 40 players; a private one is joined by its five-letter code, if you have one, up to 4. Multiplayer is for players 18 and over, under the Terms: share the walls, respect other people’s pieces, no spam, ads or hate.

FAQ

Is it free?

Yes. Free to play, with no ads and no in-game purchases. If you like it, you can buy the author a coffee on Ko-fi.

Does the paint drip?

Yes. Hold the can in one spot too long and the paint runs down the wall: use it for a raw look, or keep the can moving for clean lines.

Does my art stay?

In a public session, paint is kept on the server, so the roof keeps growing as people come and go. You can also save your paint to a file and load it back later.

What inspired it?

Bombing!! 2, Marc Ecko’s Getting Up, and Daisuke Tajima’s pen-and-ink cities in Beyond the Lines.

Can I see the source code?

Yes: it’s on GitHub, under the Hidden Roof Source-Available License.
