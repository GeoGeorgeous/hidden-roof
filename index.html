Hidden Roof, a browser-based first-person graffiti game

Hidden Roof is a free first-person graffiti game for desktop browsers. You climb onto the rooftops of a city drawn in ink, and the only color in it is the paint you bring. There are no enemies and no goals: find your tools, paint whatever you like, scrub it off, paint again. Play alone, or bring friends into a multiplayer session and paint the same roof together.
